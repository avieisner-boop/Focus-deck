How it works

Masked, verified, accountable.

🕸️

1 · Approval is everything

Nothing is public. Sellers approve brokers; sellers and brokers approve buyers. Every relationship carries its own terms — minimums, price floors, communication rules, identity reveal.

🎭

2 · Masked, not anonymous-wild-west

Every business trades as a verified storefront alias with a ratings history that can't be reset. The platform always knows who's behind the mask — counterparties don't, until the owner chooses.

⚖️

3 · Someone owns every order

Whoever owns the buyer relationship owns the order — fulfillment, delivery, disputes. Escrowed payments split automatically per hop when delivery is confirmed.
